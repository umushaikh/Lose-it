Photo estimate

Take a photo or pick one from your camera roll. A photo gives a rough estimate — portion size is hard to judge from an image — so you get to check the numbers before it's logged.

This one needs a quick one-time setup. Photo recognition runs on Claude's AI, and your app has no server of its own — so it uses your own Anthropic account directly.

  1. Go to console.anthropic.com and sign up.
  2. Add a little credit (billing → the minimum is a few dollars, and each photo costs around a penny).
  3. Open API Keys → Create Key, and copy it.
  4. Paste it into Goals → Photo estimates in this app, then press Save.

The key is saved only on this phone, never leaves it except to talk to Anthropic, and is left out of your backups. Everything else in the app works without it.
